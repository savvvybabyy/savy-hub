Letters to a Black Woman

Sister, I'm putting together something from the heart. A year of letters — 365 of them, one a day — written by women like us, for a Black woman who needs to hear them. Write the letter you once needed to read: about healing, starting over, loving yourself, raising your babies, finding your faith again, or just making it through the night. This isn't a business thing. It's a mission thing — women reaching back for women.

Where the money goes

Proceeds from book sales help cover the cost of producing these books — printing, contributor copies, and the gift cards we send our writers as a thank-you — and the rest helps fund Her Rise Academy, our community where women get help finding work-from-home jobs, grants and funding opportunities, credit improvement help, business resources, money education, and a sisterhood that actually shows up for each other. When you buy a book, you're not just buying a book. You're helping another woman rise.

Books are titled by topic and released in the order topics fill up. They'll be sold in the Mela Reads section of Mela Finds — Mela Reads is coming soon.

Submit your letter

One letter per lady per month. You may submit again in a new month. Duplicate submissions are removed. Submissions are private — your letter is never posted publicly. How we handle submissions: see our Letters privacy section.
