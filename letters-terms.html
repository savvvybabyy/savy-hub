Everything in one place, up front. Amounts and terms are shown here publicly — they can change, and the signed contributor agreement is final.

How submissions work

Submissions are blind and open: anyone may submit a letter. Amarosa's team reviews every submission and decides selections. Submitting a letter guarantees nothing — not selection, not publication, not payment. The contributor agreement goes to selected contributors after selection, and it carries the final terms.

Books are titled by topic — never by month — and are sold exclusively in the Mela Reads section of Mela Finds. Every book opens with a foreword by a guest writer and closes with a letter from Amarosa herself, delivered in a real sealed envelope. The best letters will also be considered for a Deluxe keepsake edition with real sealed envelopes.

Publishing & release schedule

PUBLISHING & RELEASE SCHEDULE — Letters to a Black Woman is published by Savy Publishing on a schedule we set. Digital (PDF) editions are released up to twice a month, with the occasional surprise release; one topic is selected for print each month. Books are released in the order topics fill with selected letters — not by calendar — and topics may be added or removed at any time. The publishing schedule, release order, and format of any book may change at any time without notice. Being selected does not guarantee a publication date.

Contributor compensation — the thank-you package

Letters to a Black Woman is a mission project, built on healing and sisterhood. There is no fee to submit a letter, and submitting a letter does not guarantee selection or payment. Compensation is paid only for a letter that is selected and published in a book in the series, as follows:

1. $25 Visa gift card — PRINTED EDITIONS ONLY

The $25 Visa gift card is paid only for letters published in a printed edition of a book in the series — the paperback or the Limited Edition hardcover. If your letter is published in a printed edition, The Savy Group LLC will send you a Visa gift card in the amount of twenty-five dollars ($25), delivered electronically 7–10 days after that printed edition's published date, provided all conditions of your contributor agreement have been met — it is not paid at submission, not paid at selection, and not paid in advance of publication. Publication in a digital (PDF) edition alone does not earn the gift card. If your letter appears in a digital edition first and is later published in a printed edition, the gift card is earned at the printed publication. It is a Visa gift card, not a cash payment. Gift cards are sent to the name and contact information you provide to us; please keep that information current so your card reaches you.

2. Your own affiliate link — 20% of every sale

Every contributor whose letter is published — including letters published in a digital (PDF) edition — will receive her own personal affiliate link and/or promo code for the book her letter appears in, pointing to that book's page in the Mela Reads section of Mela Finds. You may share your link wherever you promote the book, and you will earn twenty percent (20%) of the price paid on every qualifying sale made through your link — on any edition of the book (digital, paperback, or Limited Edition hardcover), calculated on the amount the buyer actually pays. For example, that's $2.00 on the $9.98 digital edition, $3.78 on the $18.89 paperback, and $5.98 on the $29.88 Limited Edition hardcover. One exception: no commission is paid on a sale redeemed with the series' 50%-off digital coupon — coupon sales are commission-free.

3. Contributor copy

You will receive one contributor copy of the book your letter appears in, at no charge to you — a PDF for a digital edition, a paperback for a print edition.

4. Your name in print

Your name, your short bio, and your link/social handle, as provided by you, will be printed with your letter in the book, so every reader knows whose words she is reading. Exception: letters published in Bare Soul, the anonymous volume, are printed unsigned by design; if your letter appears in Bare Soul, your name and bio will not be printed with it, but your affiliate link and contributor copy are still yours — and your gift card is yours if that volume is published in a printed edition — handled privately between you and The Savy Group LLC.

5. What is not paid

No compensation is due for a letter that is submitted but not published in a book. By submitting, you grant The Savy Group LLC permission to use your letter for any purpose of this project — including any edition of the books, the Deluxe keepsake edition, marketing and promotion, and reading letters aloud, live or online — whether or not your letter is published. Use of an unpublished letter in marketing or promotion does not create a payment obligation. Promotional use is a hard requirement of this project; there is no opt-out. The thank-you package described above (gift card, affiliate link and its 20% commission, contributor copy, and printed credit) is the full and only compensation for a published letter; no royalties, salary, or other payment is owed beyond it.

6. Who pays and when

All contributor payments and packages are provided by The Savy Group LLC, publisher of record, publishing as Savy Publishing. The gift card is delivered electronically 7–10 days after the printed edition containing your letter is published, if all conditions are met (a digital-only release does not trigger it); the contributor copy is provided after the release of the book containing your letter. If more than one of your letters is published — in the same book or in different books in the series — the thank-you package applies to each published letter.

Selection, ownership & rules

  • You keep ownership of your letter (copyright stays yours). Your letter must be your own original writing — fiction is welcome; truthfulness is not required, originality is. Never name real, identifiable people in a way that harms them.
  • Selection flow: chosen contributors are alerted before print only under a signed confidentiality agreement (NDA). They review their final edited draft, choose a font from a curated menu, and sign the final contributor agreement electronically (DocuSign) BEFORE print. The signed agreement carries the FINAL terms.
  • Private details stay private: mailing address and government ID are collected only from selected contributors, privately, through the signed agreement — never on the public form.
  • Form rules: legal name required; you must be 18 or older; one letter per lady per month (you may submit again in a new month); duplicate submissions are removed.
  • Length: about 500–600 words, hard maximum 650, following the five-step arc on the More Details page.
