Books with a heartbeat. Savy Publishing exists to put healing in women's hands — real letters, real stories, and a sisterhood that shows up on every page.

Our first series: Letters to a Black Woman

365 letters — one a day for a year — written by women, for a Black woman who needs to hear them. Each book is titled by its topic: healing after heartbreak, faith through the fire, single mama strong, grief and goodbye, money and getting back up, and more. Books are released in the order their topics fill up, and every one opens with a foreword from a guest writer and closes with a letter from Amarosa herself.

This series is a mission before it is anything else — healing and sisterhood first. It is presented by Amarosa Daily and published by Savy Publishing.

Where the money goes

Proceeds from book sales help cover the cost of producing these books — printing, contributor copies, and the gift cards we send our writers as a thank-you — and the rest helps fund Her Rise Academy, our community where women get help finding work-from-home jobs, grants and funding opportunities, credit improvement help, business resources, money education, and a sisterhood that actually shows up for each other. When you buy a book, you're not just buying a book. You're helping another woman rise.
