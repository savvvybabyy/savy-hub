Letters to a Black Woman

More Details — How to Write Your Letter

The heartbeat of this whole series

Every letter in Letters to a Black Woman says the same thing underneath, in 365 different voices: "This happened to me too — and this is how I got through it." Whatever your topic, your letter walks the same road:

  • Meet her where she is — open in your real moment, the honest one.
  • Tell it true — what happened. (No graphic detail — the weight, not the wounds on display.)
  • THE TURN — what shifted. A decision, a prayer, a day you chose different. Every letter has one.
  • How you got through it — one or two real things that actually helped. This is the gold another woman will hold onto.
  • Leave her lifted — close speaking TO the woman reading: an affirmation, an encouragement, a truth she can carry. Every letter ends upbeat and affirming. We do not leave a sister in the pit — we hand her the way out we found.

Length reminder: about 500–600 words, never more than 650. Your own words only.

The 20 topics — your lane, stay in it

1. Begin Again

Starting over: after a divorce, a job loss, a move, an empty nest, a whole life reset. Stay on: the decision to begin and the first steps. Your turn: the moment you stopped mourning the old life and touched the new one.

2. The Letter I Needed at My Lowest

Write TO the woman sitting where you once sat. Stay on: speaking to her, not just telling your story. Your turn: what you know now that you would hand back to yourself then.

3. Healing After Heartbreak

Romantic heartbreak, worked through. Stay on: YOUR healing. No naming, no bashing — this is not a tell-all. Your turn: the day you felt like yourself again.

4. Dear Mama

To a mother: gratitude, apology, forgiveness, or the words never said. Stay on: one letter, one mama, one thread. Your turn: it resolves in love or peace — even the hard ones land gentle.

5. Single Mama Strong

Raising them on your own. Stay on: the real weight AND the real wins, and at least one practical how-you-did-it another mama can copy. Your turn: what your strength actually looks like day to day.

6. Faith Through the Fire

Faith tested by a real fire: sickness, loss, fear, waiting on God. Stay on: testimony, not sermon — your story, not a lecture. Your turn: what the fire taught your faith.

7. Forgive Them, Free You

Forgiveness as setting YOURSELF free. Stay on: your release (forgiveness does not require reconciliation, and you don't have to name anyone). Your turn: what freedom feels like on the other side.

8. Love Yourself Out Loud

Self-love, confidence, body, worth. Stay on: celebration — claiming yourself at this age, this size, this season. Your turn: how you talk to yourself now.

9. Grief & Goodbye

Losing someone you love. Stay on: honest grief and how you carry them now. Gentle hope is the turn — not forced cheer, just proof that love and life continue.

10. I Survived That Too

Survival: illness, abuse, accidents, hard systems, the thing that was supposed to take you out. Stay on: survival and the road back; no graphic detail. Your turn: "I survived — and here is how I live now."

11. Money, Hustle & Getting Back Up

Financial falls and comebacks: debt, eviction, starting the business, losing it, rebuilding. Stay on: the fall AND the rebuild, with ONE practical move that actually worked. Your turn: where you stand now vs. then.

12. Sisterhood

The women who showed up: friends, sisters, aunties, strangers who became sisters. Stay on: celebrate them (first names only, or no names). Your turn: what real sisterhood taught you about showing up.

13. To My Younger Self

Advice across the years. Stay on: warm and honest, not regret-heavy. Your turn: what you'd tell her to stop fearing — and what to start sooner.

14. Letters From the Elders

For our seasoned sisters (60+): wisdom written down for the women coming behind you. Stay on: legacy — the lesson of a lifetime in one letter. Your turn: the blessing you leave them.

15. New Season, New Me

Transitions: new decade, new city, new calling, kids grown, body changing. Stay on: stepping INTO, not just out of. Your turn: what this season gets from you that the last one didn't.

16. For the Woman in Waiting

Waiting seasons: for love, for a baby, for the breakthrough, for the answer. Stay on: how to wait WELL — living fully now, not pausing your life. Your turn: what the waiting is building in you.

17. Joy Is Your Birthright

Reclaiming joy, laughter, softness, delight after hard years. Stay on: joy as a decision and a practice — playful is welcome here. Your turn: how you protect your joy now.

18. When Nobody Clapped

Doing it unsupported: no cheers, no help, no recognition. Stay on: the work and the worth, not bitterness. Your turn: learning to clap for yourself — and meaning it.

19. Bare Soul (Anonymous)

The raw page. Say the thing you would never sign. Stay on: raw, unfiltered, off your chest — the only limits are no real names and no graphic detail. Your turn: even here, land it — "this is how I'm getting through it," even if you're still mid-storm. Bare Soul prints with no name and no bio; your identity stays private with us.

20. To the Woman I'm Becoming

Future-facing: the woman you are growing into. Stay on: declaration, not wishes — speak her into the room. Your turn: the promise you are making yourself, out loud.
