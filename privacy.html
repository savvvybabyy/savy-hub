Privacy

How we handle your information

Plain and simple: we collect what we need to do the work, we use it for that work, and we don't sell your information.

The basics

  • What we collect: the information you send us through this site's forms — your name, email, organization, and the details of your request — plus anything you email us directly.
  • How we use it: to respond to you, prepare quotes, deliver the services you purchase, and keep track of our work together.
  • What we never do: we do not sell your personal information. Payments are processed by Square; we never see or store your full card number.
  • Questions: email amarosa@thesavygroup.com.

Letters submissions — how yours is handled

  • Stored for the project: when you submit a letter to Letters to a Black Woman, your submission — your topic, letter title, legal name, social handle, bio, and the letter itself — is stored by The Savy Group LLC for the life of the project and given a submission ID.
  • Private, never public: submissions are not posted publicly. Your letter is read by Amarosa's team for selection only.
  • Blind review: selections are decided by Amarosa's team. Submitting a letter guarantees nothing — not selection, not publication, not payment.
  • What we do NOT collect on the public form: no mailing address and no government ID are collected when you submit. Those are collected later, privately, only from selected contributors, through the signed contributor agreement.
  • Bare Soul anonymity: for the anonymous Bare Soul volume, your real name and contact details are collected privately with your submission and kept private — they are never printed with your letter.
  • Unselected letters: letters that are not selected stay under consideration for future books in the series, under the same terms you agreed to when you submitted.
