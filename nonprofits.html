What the package covers

Included

Grant requests up to $10,000 with online applications or short proposals — corporate giving, community foundations, small foundations — 2 per month.

Quoted à la carte

Grants of $10,001–$50,000, and any grant requiring a full narrative proposal, logic model, or multi-year budget.

Special quote

Grants over $50,000, government RFPs (city / county / state / federal), capital campaigns, and multi-year proposals.

The $147 package fee never covers a $100,000 grant application. If it isn't on this page, it's priced by special quote or à la carte add-on — no exceptions priced off the sheet.
